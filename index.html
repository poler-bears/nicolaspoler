About

I am an artist and educator
based in San Francisco, California.

I have art, science, and endless passion for learning and teaching in my heart.

I was raised in San Francisco, steeped in Venezuelan culture and an international community. I have worked with kids and teens in theater education for almost a decade.

Having graduated from UNCSA with a BFA in Acting, I am ecstatic to bring the artistry and culture I have absorbed back to my community in the Bay Area.

I am especially interested in stories dealing with Latinness, queerness, religion, and the absurdity of reality.

  • Ran a theater company as Artistic Director
  • Fluent in Spanish and French
  • Classically trained pianist

I look forward to meeting you!
